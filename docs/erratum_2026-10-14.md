**Kind:** corrections to how recorded results are described. Nothing is re-evaluated. No verdict,

## E1. "0.82 against 0.66, on the same sample" compares two different windows

**Where:** `SPEC.md` §1, the earnings bullet.

**Record:** the earnings-announcement + volatility-scaled momentum combination's in-house net Sharpe
of 0.818 covers 2023-04-03 → 2026-06-12, 802 sessions
(`results/2026-09-earnings/replication/replication.json`, `combination.in_house`). Volatility-scaled
momentum's 0.663 covers 2022-09-29 → 2026-06-12, 929 sessions
(`results/2026-09-vol-momentum/summary.json`, `in_house.scaled`). The source evaluation
(`docs/earnings_read_2026-09-24.md`) used "same sample" to mean *the same data the idea was found
on, so it cannot promote*, not a matched comparison.

**Effect on verdicts:** none. **Corrected wording:** both figures are in-sample, over different
windows (802 and 929 sessions), and neither is a forecast.

spread. **Effect on verdicts:** none; the certification's verdict and B1's DSR of 0.191 against 17
trials stand.

## E3. The momentum reference Sharpe (0.465) excludes dividends

**Record:** `results/demotion_b1/claim_packet.json` records
`data_convention = split_adjusted_open_close_price_return_no_dividends`.
`docs/program_review_2026-07.md` measures the book's dividend flow at −36.1 bp/yr (momentum's
short leg out-yields its long leg), which puts a total-return reading at about **0.434**.
The engine can credit dividends as cash; the reference record did not. **Effect on verdicts:** none
recorded; the public replication-route figures use total-return public series and are unaffected.

## E4. The two combination priors were computed on different bases

The earnings combination's prior, 0.474, is 0.4 × its full-window public Sharpe (1.185,
1972-08 → 2024-12), because the post-2015-04 common window spanned 9.74 years, under the ten-year
rule (`replication.json`, `combination.prior.basis = haircut_full_window`). That exceeds each part's
own post-publication Sharpe (0.410 for the announcement return; 0.465, or 0.401 through 2025-12,
for volatility-scaled momentum). The fundamentals combination used its post-2015 Sharpe (0.393).
Both follow SPEC §4, but they are not comparable, and the earnings prior is the more generous.
**Effect on verdicts:** none; both combinations fail.
